Prairie Walk, Sid Richardson Tract

Solo version of the FWAS guided walk · 3rd Friday, Feb–Apr only (FWAS)

🚗 Drive to the trailhead

Sid Richardson Tract, Benbrook Lake — Old Granbury Rd, Crowley. SE shore of Benbrook Lake, just east of Mustang Park. Car-pool — limited parking.

Maps from here

🚶 Walk the route

Wander the natural-surface prairie trails on the southeast shore of Benbrook Lake. Some wander off-trail; it's a prairie, not a park.

The pin is the tract area — explore the prairie trails on foot.

Walking directions

Good to know

  • 3rd Friday, Feb–Apr ONLY (summer heat + Sept–Jan hunting season close it)
  • Feb/Mar 9:00 AM, Apr 8:00 AM; about 1.5 hours
  • Natural surface — boots, long pants, hat, sunscreen, bug spray
  • No facilities; bring water

eBird hotspot

Benbrook Lake (general area) — 315 species all-time. Check recent sightings before you go.
