Parr Park, Grapevine

Solo version of the FWAS guided walk · Quarterly Sundays (FWAS)

🚗 Drive to the trailhead

Rotary Pavilion parking — 3010 Parr Ln, Grapevine. Park by the Rotary Pavilion.

Maps from here

🚶 Walk the route

From the Rotary Pavilion, follow the Bear Creek Trail through the park along Bear Creek — about 2.5 miles round trip, paved, level, and shady.

Standard walk stays in Parr Park (~2.5 mi round trip). The walking pin points toward the ambitious extension: Pool Rd east to Bear Creek Park on Hwy 360, 6.2 miles round trip.

Walking directions

Good to know

  • Quarterly Sundays, 8:00 AM
  • ~2.5 miles round trip: paved, level, shady bottom woodlands along Bear Creek
  • Bathrooms in the park
  • Very birdy in migration — try the Rock Art Trail area too

eBird hotspot

Parr Park — 142 species all-time. Check recent sightings before you go.
