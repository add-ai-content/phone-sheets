Friday Feathers, Kelly Park (Arlington)

Solo version of the FWAS guided walk · 1st Friday monthly (FWAS)

🚗 Drive to the trailhead

Creekside Dr parking lot (dead end) — From Arkansas Ln turn north on Woodside Dr, then east on Creekside to the dead end. Bigger lot, quieter trails than the main entrance.

Maps from here

🚶 Walk the route

Follow the linear paved trail system from the Creekside Dr lot, out and back. The north section stays away from the park's busier areas.

The pin is the park itself — out and back on the linear trails.

Walking directions

Good to know

  • 1st Friday monthly: 7:30 AM Jun–Sep, 8:30 AM Oct–May
  • Easy walking on paved trails
  • Main entrance (if you prefer it): 4195 W Arkansas Ln
  • Bring water

eBird hotspot

Kelley Park, Arlington — 175 species all-time. Check recent sightings before you go.
