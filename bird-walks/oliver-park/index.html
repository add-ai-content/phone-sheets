Oliver Park, Mansfield

Solo version of the FWAS guided walk · 3rd Tuesday, Sept–May (FWAS)

🚗 Drive to the trailhead

Elmer W. Oliver Nature Park entrance — 1650 Matlock Rd, Mansfield. Park at the main lot.

Maps from here

🚶 Walk the route

Loop the boardwalk and crushed-granite paths past the pond, creek, meadows, overlooks, and wooded areas. Several habitats in one easy walk.

The pin is the park itself — follow the boardwalk and granite paths.

Walking directions

Good to know

  • 3rd Tuesday monthly, Sept–May, 8:30–10:30 AM
  • Easy walking: boardwalk and crushed-granite paths
  • Habitats: pond, creek, meadows, overlooks, woods
  • Bring water and snacks
