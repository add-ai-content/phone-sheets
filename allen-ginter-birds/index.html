Reference pictures are the larger N37 cards, from the Metropolitan Museum of Art's Open Access Burdick Collection (public domain) — the N4 and N37 cards carry the same artwork. The sets are Allen & Ginter's Birds of America: N4 from 1888; N37 dated 1890 by TCDB/PSA and 1888 by the Met. The cards themselves are unnumbered — the numbers here are checklist positions.
