Stop 1
Dallas World Aquarium
1801 N Griffin St, Dallas, TX 75202
Shot list — BIRDS (rainforest canopy, top of Orinoco exhibit): • Shoebill stork — Borneo entrance exhibit. Statue-still for minutes; no flash; slow shutter fine. • Andean cock-of-the-rock — absurdly orange; may sit high in foliage, watch exposure. • Resplendent quetzal — male's tail streamers; canopy light is dim, push ISO. • Toco toucan + 5 more toucan species — active; burst mode. • Harpy eagle — huge; likely perched high, bring the long end. • Scarlet ibis & scarlet macaw, hummingbirds, tanagers — hummers need fast shutter. Shot list — MAMMALS: • Two-toed & three-toed sloths — canopy; motionless, easy. • Giant river otters — fast and playful; burst mode. • Antillean manatees — underwater viewing; big slow subjects. Tips: personal photos OK, no flash around animals. Shoebill viewing reported limited — confirm at entry.
