LosslessCut: the happy pathField Guide #1 · cut the good bit out of a bird-cam clip, no re-encoding, no fuss

LosslessCut does one job: cut pieces out of a video without re-encoding it. No quality loss, no waiting around. Think of it as a paper cutter for video. Here's the whole happy path, start to finish.

1. Drop the clip in

Drag your video file straight onto the LosslessCut window. Or press Ctrl+O and pick it. That's it — it's loaded.

2. Find the good bit

Press Space to play and pause. Nudge frame by frame with , and . — the arrow keys jump a full second, so save those for coarse moves. When the bird does the thing, stop.

This is your "scrub and eyeball" step. Take your time — nothing is cut until you say so.

3. Mark the keeper

Park the playhead where the good bit starts and press I. Move to where it ends and press O.

The marked stretch lights up on the timeline. That's your segment — the part that survives.

More than one good bit? Press + for a new segment, then I / O again. Each segment becomes its own file — unless you flip Export mode from Separate files to Merge cuts, which glues them into one.

4. Export it

Press E. An overview pops up — press Export again to confirm. The cut file lands next to your original in seconds.

Your original is never touched. LosslessCut only ever writes new files.

5. Grab a still (bonus)

See a frame you'd want as a photo or a reel cover? Pause on it and press C (or the camera button). Snapshot saved, done.

The one gotcha that matters: normal cuts snap to the nearest keyframe, so your in/out points can land a few frames off from where you marked. For bird clips you won't notice. If you ever need frame-exact precision, switch the cut mode to Smart Cut — it re-encodes just the tiny sliver around the cut and leaves the rest lossless.

(This is also why scrubbing jumps on files with sparse keyframes — the playhead can only land cleanly on keyframes. Same animal, different symptom.)
Cutting out the boring middle instead? Mark the boring bit with I / O, then click the yin-yang ☯ symbol. It flips the logic: everything except your marked segments gets exported. Handy for removing a dead stretch from the middle of a clip.
